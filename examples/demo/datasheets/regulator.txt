Output voltage: 3.3 V nominal. This teaching example assumes exactly 3.3 V output.
Ground current maximum is 0.005 A at the scenario's input and load conditions.
Junction-to-ambient thermal resistance is 100 K/W for the declared teaching PCB.
Maximum recommended junction temperature is 125 degC.
Pin 1 is VIN, pin 2 is GND and pin 3 is VOUT.

For this simplified linear regulator, P = (VIN - VOUT) * IOUT + VIN * IQ.
Estimate junction temperature as TJ = TA + P * thetaJA under steady-state conditions.
The thermal resistance depends strongly on PCB copper and airflow.
Output capacitor ESR, dropout, current limiting and transient response are not specified.
